# 📚 PaperPilot

**PaperPilot** is a smart tool for managing, organizing, and automating research papers — helping students, researchers, and writers stay on top of their reading and citations.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📥 **Paper Collection** — Add and organize research papers in one place
- 🔍 **Search & Filter** — Quickly find papers by title, author, or keyword
- 🏷️ **Tagging System** — Categorize papers with custom tags
- 📝 **Notes & Highlights** — Save key points from each paper
- 📤 **Export** — Export your library or citations easily
- ⚡ **Fast & Lightweight** — Simple setup, quick performance
